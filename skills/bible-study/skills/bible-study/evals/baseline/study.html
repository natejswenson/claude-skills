01 / When & where

When the book was written

Often dated AD 85-95 and associated with Ephesus; this dates the Gospel, not the events. [2][9]

Traditionally John the apostle; the author is unnamed. Dating is uncertain; some Christian scholars argue for before AD 70. [2][9]

When the events happened

During Jesus' ministry, after the John 2 Passover scene and before the Baptist's imprisonment (3:24). No exact year is given. [1][8]

What was happening around it

Nicodemus is a Pharisee and Jewish leader; purification and baptism matter here. On the later dating, readers lived after Jerusalem's destruction in AD 70. [1][8][9]

John 2 closes with responses to signs. Nicodemus starts there; Jesus leads him toward new life. [1][8][3]

02 / What it means

God gives new life through the Spirit and eternal life through faith in his Son. [1][3]

3:1-13 · Receive new birth

Knowledge cannot produce new birth. The Spirit gives it; anew can also mean again or from above. [1][4]

3:14-21 · Believe in the Son

The lifted serpent points to the Son. God gives life to believers; light exposes evil and rejection. [1][4]

3:22-36 · Make room for Jesus

John rejoices as people turn to Jesus. The Son speaks God's words; belief brings life, rejection leaves judgment. [1]

Interpretive note. Water and Spirit (3:5): Christians differ between readings including baptism and Ezekiel's promised cleansing. [4][5]

03 / Read alongside

Ezekiel 36:25-27 · Cleansing & a new heart

God promises cleansing, a new heart, and his Spirit: an Old Testament background for John 3:5-10. [5][4]

Numbers 21:4-9 · Look & live

Those bitten look at the bronze serpent and live. Jesus explicitly draws this connection in John 3:14-15. [6][1]

John 20:30-31 · Why John wrote

John writes to nurture belief in Jesus as Messiah and Son, and life in his name. [7]

04 / Study together

Read 8 min / explore 12 / discuss 20 / pray 5

  1. Read John 3 aloud. What surprises Nicodemus? What does Jesus say the Spirit does (3:1-13)?
  2. Read Numbers 21:4-9. How does it illuminate John 3:14-18? How do love and judgment fit together?
  3. Compare hiding from light (3:19-21) with John's joy (3:29-30). Where could you practice honesty or humility this week?

05 / Put it into practice

Choose one response: pray about a hidden struggle, seek a trusted Christian's help, or serve without seeking credit. Revisit it next meeting.

Pray
