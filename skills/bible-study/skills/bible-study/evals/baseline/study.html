Life from above. John 3

What does it mean to receive new life and put Jesus first?

Begin · 3 min Pray for understanding. Read each section aloud. Support your answers with a verse.

01 / New birth

Read 3:1-13 aloud · 11 min

  1. Observe · What confuses Nicodemus? What does the Spirit do (vv. 3–8)?
  2. Interpret · Why does Nicodemus need new birth despite his knowledge?

Compare Ezekiel 36:25-27. What echoes do you hear in John 3:5–10?

Notes / verse

02 / Love & light

Read 3:14-21 aloud · 11 min

  1. Observe · What does God give? Why do people avoid the light (vv. 16–21)?
  2. Interpret · How do God’s love and judgment fit together here?

Compare Numbers 21:4-9. What does this help explain in John 3:14–15?

Notes / verse

03 / Humble witness

Read 3:22-36 aloud · 11 min

  1. Observe · Contrast the disciples’ concern (v. 26) with John’s joy (vv. 27–30).
  2. Interpret · What does the bridegroom picture reveal about John’s role?

Compare John 20:30-31. How does John 3:31–36 serve this purpose?

Notes / verse

Context to keep nearby

Writing & authorship

Often dated AD 85–95, associated with Ephesus. [2][9]

Traditionally John the apostle; the Gospel is unsigned. Uncertain: some Christian scholars argue for a date before AD 70. [2][9]

Setting of the events

Jesus’ ministry: after John 2’s Passover, before John’s imprisonment (3:24). Exact year unstated. [1][8]

Nicodemus: Pharisee and Jewish leader. Purification and baptism feature here. Late dating places readers after Jerusalem’s destruction (AD 70). [1][8][9]

John 2’s signs and belief lead into Nicodemus’s visit (3:2). [1][8][3]

Meaning to reflect on

God gives new life through the Spirit and eternal life through faith in his Son. [1][3]

3:1-13 New birth comes through the Spirit. [1][4]

3:14-21 God gives his Son for life; rejecting him brings judgment. [1][4]

3:22-36 John rejoices in Jesus’ prominence; the Son speaks God’s words. [1]

Christians differ on “water and Spirit” (3:5), including baptism and promised cleansing. [4][5]

Connections

Ezekiel 36:25-27 God promises cleansing, a new heart, and his Spirit. [5][4]

Numbers 21:4-9 The bitten Israelites look at the bronze serpent and live. [6][1]

John 20:30-31 John writes so readers may believe and have life in Jesus. [7]
