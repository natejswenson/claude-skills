I built an AI running coach on my own Garmin data, and the persona was a real decision: Richard Simmons or David Goggins.

I went Goggins: warmth near zero, never satisfied. It reads my sleep, training load, and body battery before I do, and it doesn't congratulate me for showing up.

It caught a soft run and called it soft. The next workout was the one I'd planned, not the one I'd settled for.

Same data either way; the persona decides what your agent lets slide. If you're building one, cast that role on purpose.
