Language models are bad at the word "not."

I told my AI coach, in capital letters, never to name a letter grade. It named one on about 3% of lines. When I regenerated the answer, it named the same grade again.

Mine was worse than the usual "don't." My prompt printed every grade the model was writing about ("Pace: F, Distance: D-") one line above the rule forbidding it. The model wasn't disobeying. It was completing.

Anthropic's own advice is to tell the model what to do, not what to avoid. So I took the letters out of the context and passed the meaning instead ("Pace: missed badly"). Nothing left to echo, and the leak went to zero.

If your model won't stop doing something, check whether you're the one putting it in front of it.
